Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut

The koenigsegg Jesko Absolut is a Swedish ultra high performance hyper car built for maximum straight line speed.

Need for Top Speed

The Jesko Absolut is the fastest Koenigsegg ever built, with a theoretical top speed of over 330 mph (531 km/h).
This makes it one of the fastest production cars in the world, designed for enthusiasts who crave extreme speed and performance.
The Jesko Absolut is powered by a twin-turbocharged V8 engine that produces an astonishing amount of horsepower, allowing it to achieve incredible acceleration and top speeds.
Its aerodynamic design and advanced engineering contribute to its ability to reach such high velocities, making it a true hypercar in every sense of the word.

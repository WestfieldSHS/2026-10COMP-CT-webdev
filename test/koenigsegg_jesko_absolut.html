Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut

The koenigsegg Jesko Absolut is a Swedish ultra high performance hyper car built for maximum straight line speed.
The koenigsegg is just as powerful as its track-focused
sibling, the Jesko is the more
seamless and stealthy of the two. Every
surface element is constructed to
reduce drag or surrounding turbulence
while increasing high-speed stability.

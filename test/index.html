supercar and hypercars are like the upgraded and ascention of normal cars. Supercars are high-performance, street-legel sports cars that are desgined for extreme speeds, and are usually made to look exotic, expensive.

What is classified as a hyper / super car?

The term "Hypercar" is used to describe the most extreme and high-performance vehicles in the automotive world or the world of cars.
Hypercars are typically limited-production, ultra-expensive, and technologically advanced machines that push the boundaries of speed, aerodynamics, and engineering.
They often feature hybrid or electric powertrains, cutting-edge materials, and innovative design elements.
Hypercars are designed to deliver unparalleled performance on both the road and the track, making them the pinnacle of automotive achievement.

SuperCars and Hypercars

supercar and hypercars are like the upgraded and ascention of normal cars. Supercars are high-performance, street-legel sports cars that are desgined for extreme speeds, and are usually made to look exotic, expensive.
A supercar is an elite, street-legal sports car that offers extreme performance, striking aesthetics, and exclusive craftsmanship. While there is no rigid rulebook, these vehicles transcend regular sports cars by utilizing race-derived engineering and exotic materials to deliver thrilling speed and handling.

Engineering & Performance Oddities

Fuel Consumption: Some high-performance cars, like the Dodge Hellcat, have injectors that can drain a massive amount of fuel, with some estimates suggesting intense, constant usage could exhaust an immense amount of fuel over a year.
Extreme Heat: The cabin (the space where the F1 driver occupies) of a Supercar racer can reach up to 65°C, causing drivers to lose about 3kg in sweat per race
